About

I am a third-year PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering at NYU, advised by Martín Farach-Colton. My research focuses on bridging theory and practice: applying rigorous theoretical tools to algorithmic problems that arise in real-world computing. I am particularly motivated by problems in indexing, retrieval, and data structures for modern machine learning systems.

Before NYU, I was at the Weizmann Institute of Science, where I worked with Amir Abboud on graph algorithms.
